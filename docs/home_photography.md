# SPACE Home photography

The Home header uses four illustrative photographs. They do not depict actual SPACE facilities or members. Each was downloaded from an individual free photo page, resized, and saved as a WebP under `assets/home-photos/`. The site serves the files itself so members do not contact a third-party image host.

| Learning | `arduino-lab.webp` | [Vishnu Mohanan, Arduino and breadboard](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-circuit-board-with-a-soldering-tool-attached-to-it-0iRfjG7IGeo) |
